## AudioScheduler

The `AudioScheduler` coordinates timed playback with the `AudioEngine` (see `setScheduler` on [AudioEngine](/api/audio/audio-engine)). For the ESP32 dual-core audio pipeline, read [Audio architecture](/architecture/audio-architecture).
